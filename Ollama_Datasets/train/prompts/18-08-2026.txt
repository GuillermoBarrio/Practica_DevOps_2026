AI-fuelled equity optimism is colliding with a bond market increasingly pricing fiscal deterioration and persistent inflation, weighing on early price action.

Brent crude has moved about 3% higher since Europe went home Monday, reflecting the fresh setback to potential peace in the Middle East after Trump said he’s not interested in extending an expiring agreement with Iran. 
 
Source: Bloomberg
